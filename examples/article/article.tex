\section{Diagrams}\label{sec:diagrams}
Commutative diagrams come from \texttt{tikz-cd}, which pomtex fetches together
with its dependency \texttt{pgf}:
\[
\begin{tikzcd}
  A \arrow[r, "f"] \arrow[d, "g"'] & B \arrow[d, "h"] \\
  C \arrow[r, "k"']                 & D
\end{tikzcd}
\]

\section{Units and tables}
As shown in Section~\ref{sec:diagrams}, packages are planted on demand.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lS[table-format=3.2]}
  \toprule
  Quantity     & {Value} \\
  \midrule
  Speed        & 299.79 \\
  Acceleration &   9.81 \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The value \qty{9.81}{\metre\per\second\squared} is typeset by \texttt{siunitx}.
